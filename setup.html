Armada, setup guide for subscribers

Setting up the follower

Twenty minutes, once. After this your terminal mirrors the Armada account on its own, including while your computer is off — provided it runs on a VPS.

1What you need

  • A MetaTrader 5 account with any broker. Hedging accounts are recommended; on a netting account several bots trading the same symbol will merge into one position and your results will drift from mine.
  • A VPS, so the terminal stays connected. The MQL5 VPS built into MetaTrader (Right-click the account → Register a Virtual Server) is the cheapest route and works with this EA, since it uses no DLLs.
  • Your licence key (WSF-…) and your access token, both sent by email after subscribing. The key activates the EA, the token identifies your terminal. Do not share either — they are what your subscription unlocks.
  • The file ArmadaFollower.ex5, attached to the same email. It is not published for download and it will not trade without a valid licence key.

2Install the EA

  1. In MetaTrader 5, open File → Open Data Folder.
  2. Go into MQL5\Experts and copy ArmadaFollower.ex5 — the file attached to your welcome email — there.
  3. Back in the terminal, right-click Expert Advisors in the Navigator panel and choose Refresh. Armada Follower now appears in the list.

3Allow the connection

MetaTrader blocks outgoing web requests until you name the address yourself. This is the one step people miss, so here it is on video.

  1. Open Tools → Options → Expert Advisors.
  2. Tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL.
  3. Add the signal host exactly as written in your welcome email, for example http://217.160.64.92:8080. Include the port, no trailing slash.
  4. Add https://api.wisfli.com on a second line. That is where the EA checks your licence.
  5. Press OK.

Copy it, don't type it. A single wrong character means the EA never reaches the signal and your chart stays silent. If you are on the MQL5 VPS, set the whitelist on your local terminal first, then migrate to the virtual server so the setting travels with it.

4Attach and configure

  1. Open any chart. One chart, one symbol — it does not matter which, the EA does not read that chart. Never run two copies of the follower on the same account.
  2. Drag Armada Follower onto it.
  3. On the Common tab, tick Allow Algo Trading.
  4. On the Inputs tab, fill in the fields below, then press OK.
  5. Make sure the Algo Trading button in the toolbar is green.

A smiling face in the top-right corner of the chart means the EA is running. Whether it is connected is shown in the panel it draws on the chart.

5Every setting explained

Max entry slippageHow far price may have moved since the signal opened before the follower gives up on that trade. 15 pips is a sensible default.
Max spreadSkip a copy if your broker's spread is wider than this at that moment. Protects you on news spikes.
Magic numberLeave as it is. It is how the EA recognises its own positions — anything you trade by hand is left untouched.

6Position size and risk

This is the only decision that really matters, so the EA gives you seven ways to make it. Pick one mode and one number; the panel on the chart then shows in plain words what it is doing on every trade.

ModeWhat the value meansUse it when
Balance ratio1.0 takes the same percentage risk as the Armada account, 0.5 half of it.The default, and the right answer for most people.
Equity ratioThe same, measured on equity instead of balance, so open losses shrink new trades.You want the copy to back off by itself during a drawdown.
Percent of the signal lot25 copies a quarter of whatever the signal trades.You want a fixed fraction and don't care about balances.
Multiplier2.0 doubles the signal's lot, 0.5 halves it.Same idea, expressed as a factor.
Fixed lot0.03 opens 0.03 lots every time, whatever the signal did.You want every trade the same size, full stop.
Lots per 10,0000.05 means 0.05 lots for each 10,000 of your balance.You think in lots per account size rather than in ratios.
Risk percent1.0 sizes the trade so that the signal's stop loss costs about 1% of your balance.You size by risk. Trades that arrive without a stop loss are skipped.

Whatever the mode, your broker will not go below 0.01 lots, and that floor is what bites on small accounts. A calculated 0.004 either becomes nothing or becomes 0.01 — more than double the intended size. That is what the below the broker minimum setting decides.

Under €2,000, halve the value and leave the minimum-lot setting on “skip”. Under €1,000 the rounding dominates: a normal losing streak hurts far more on your side than on the signal account, and the copy stops resembling it at all. Wait until the account is bigger rather than subscribing early.

Two other things move your results away from the signal's, and neither is a fault: your spread and commission, and your execution speed. Expect a small, permanent gap in both directions.

7Check it is working

The EA draws a panel in the corner of the chart. Within a few seconds of loading it should read Connected and show the time of the last update, refreshing every second. If the signal account has positions open and you did not skip them, they appear in your terminal within a second or two.

There is nothing else to do. Do not close positions by hand to "help" it — the follower reconciles against the signal account continuously and will reopen anything it still expects to be there.
